You must choose exactly one of the following action types for your turn:

- RESPOND_ALLIANCE: Accept or reject a pending alliance proposal from another country.
- TRADE: Initiate a trade agreement or economic exchange.
- SANCTION: Apply economic or diplomatic sanctions against another country.
- PASS: Take no specific action this turn.
